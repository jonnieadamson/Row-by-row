    Photograph the written pattern and it becomes a checklist, one row at a time. Sharp, well-lit photos read best; for a long pattern, take one photo per page.

    Photo buttons not opening?

    Some phone apps don’t let a page open the camera or photo library. Two ways round it:

    • Open this tracker in your phone’s web browser (Chrome or Safari) using the same link. When you’re signed in, your saved patterns should follow you.
    • Or copy the photo in your photos app, then paste it into the box below.

          Voice commands and reading aloud

          You can say: “row complete”, “next row”, “advance five rows”, “go back”, “go to row forty”, “read row”, “where am I”, “section finished” or “stop listening”.

          Lost your place? Match a photo of your knitting

          Photograph your knitting laid flat and the pattern is compared against it to suggest which row you’ve reached. It’s an estimate – check it against your work before moving.

          Key, colours and abbreviations

          Diagram symbols

          The diagram shows stitches in the order you work them, left to right. The outlined row is the one to work now, with rows already worked beneath it. “↻ rep” marks one repeat of the stitch pattern, not the whole row.
